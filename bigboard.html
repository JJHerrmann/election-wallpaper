How the estimate works. Once at least 5 counties are essentially complete (95%+ of precincts in), the board takes their average shift in margin since 2024 (weighted by votes) and applies it to every other county's 2024 result to estimate how the votes still out will break. Expected votes per county are its 2024 turnout scaled by the turnout seen in completed counties, allowed to differ between Democratic- and Republican-leaning counties once 8 are complete. The rough range comes from re-running the estimate on resampled sets of completed counties. Tested on past NC, VA and GA races with simulated partial counts, it landed within about 1-2 points on average once a third of counties were complete, but missed by up to 5 early on. It's arithmetic, not a model and not a race call: it ignores that early, mail and Election Day ballots report at different times and lean differently, and the first counties to finish are rarely typical. "% in" is precincts reporting as each state defines it; North Carolina counts early votes before precincts report, so early-night counties can show lots of votes with few precincts in. Results: NC State Board of Elections, Virginia Department of Elections, Georgia Secretary of State. 2024 baseline: the same offices' certified 2024 presidential results. County outlines: US Census Bureau via us-atlas.
