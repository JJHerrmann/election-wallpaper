Counts come directly from each state's official election-night feed (North Carolina, Virginia, Georgia, Iowa, Colorado), checked every few minutes on election night. States that offer no live feed, or that block automated access, are linked to their official results pages instead. Partial counts swing as different ballot types report: early and mail votes often come in first and lean differently from Election Day votes. "Winner" appears only when a state's own feed marks one.
