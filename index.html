2026 Senate, House & Governor Election Forecast Map

This live-updating map tracks every rated 2026 US Senate, House of Representatives, and gubernatorial race, refreshed daily and whenever breaking campaign news (a candidate dropping out, an indictment, a death) suggests the ratings might have moved. Switch tabs above to see President, Senate, House, or Governor; click any state or district for its candidates, party, incumbency, and latest poll.

How the ratings work

Senate and Governor ratings are a consensus of published forecaster ratings — Cook Political Report, Sabato's Crystal Ball, Inside Elections, RealClearPolitics, Silver Bulletin, Race to the WH, and others — aggregated from Wikipedia's own tracking of those forecasters' calls. This is institutional judgment, not a raw poll average, and it deliberately moves more slowly and conservatively than day-to-day polling: a single strong poll won't flip a rating category, especially in a state with a strong partisan lean. House districts are shaded by real Cook Partisan Voting Index (structural lean, not a forecast), with the ~90 genuinely competitive districts shown in full color against a muted backdrop of the ~345 safe ones. The President tab shows the certified 2024 result, since there is no 2026 presidential race.

Sourcing & freshness

All data is sourced from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA), which itself aggregates the named forecasters' published work. Nothing here is original polling or an original forecast. The page shows exactly when it was last refreshed in the summary panel above.
